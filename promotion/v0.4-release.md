ORBIT v0.4 makes the browser model independently runnable and testable, and adds three interactive examples of real model mistakes. The browser keeps the original v0.3 weights and daily leaderboard rules.

- Separate encoder, action-predictor and coordinate-probe NPZ files, exported tensor-for-tensor from the original browser model.
- A working notebook: all seven code cells execute, including a fresh small benchmark.
- NumPy inference and simulator with Python/JavaScript parity checks across all 27 plans.
- A published CPU training recipe for the action predictor, three complete 5,000-step training runs, checkpoints, validation histories and measured resource usage.
- A reproducible benchmark on 3,072 scenes per condition, with confidence intervals, three evaluation seeds, observation noise and changed impulse strength.
- A failure gallery in Russian, English and Chinese; each scene opens in the playground. Language switching now uses the corresponding real page URL while retaining game state.

The released browser model reaches 69.53% of goals in the new normal-condition benchmark. The three newly trained predictors score 71.94%, 72.56% and 71.91%; they are published as experiments and are not silently substituted into the game. These measurements use a new scene namespace, so they do not claim to reproduce the historical 67.8% exactly.

The original encoder pretraining code/data remain unavailable. New training covers the action predictor over the supplied frozen encoder, not full-system training from scratch. No real-world robotics comparison, gravity, moving target or closed-loop MPC is claimed.
