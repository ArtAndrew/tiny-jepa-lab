# ORBIT v0.7 — quick play, friend challenges and model weaknesses

[Play](https://artandrew.github.io/tiny-jepa-lab/) · [Играть](https://artandrew.github.io/tiny-jepa-lab/ru/) · [中文](https://artandrew.github.io/tiny-jepa-lab/zh/)

A small world model is more interesting when you can test its mistakes yourself. ORBIT now starts with one quick round: choose three pushes, compare your trajectory with JEPA, and try again.

- **Challenge a friend:** share a single round, a weakness experiment or a completed five-round daily run. The link carries scene settings and actions; opening it recalculates the score. No backend or account is required for this exchange.
- **Find a weakness:** adjust image noise and the target position. Succeed by hitting a target that JEPA misses. Impossible targets are identified, and a shared experiment keeps its settings fixed.
- **Get playing sooner:** a compact introduction and three-step guide come before the game; the full world-model and LLM explanations remain below it.
- All new controls and messages are available in English, Russian and Simplified Chinese, with light/dark/automatic themes.

Quick play and experiments are separate from the daily leaderboard. Challenge links are reproducible replays, not proof of the author's identity. The optional public leaderboard still uses GitHub submissions and server-side replay checks.

The active model and physics are unchanged. Its measured **74.45%** hit rate is on the 3,072-scene benchmark used to choose it from three complete training runs, not an independent post-selection estimate. [Benchmark](https://github.com/ArtAndrew/tiny-jepa-lab/blob/main/BENCHMARK.md) · [Training from scratch](https://github.com/ArtAndrew/tiny-jepa-lab/blob/main/FROM_SCRATCH.md).

По-русски: начни с одного раунда, отправь другу свой вызов или найди сцену, где попадёшь сам, а JEPA промахнётся. Вызовы работают прямо по ссылке, без регистрации. Веса модели и правила дневного рейтинга сохранены.
